Hardware Verification Blueprint (Proteus & Physical)

1. Optical Sensing Node

LDR and LED configured across sample tube. Unfiltered particles scatter light, altering resistance fed directly into ADC Channel 0.

2. RTC & Thermal Logging

DS1307 RTC communicates over I2C (SDA/SCL) providing continuous timestamping; LM35 feeds ADC Channel 1 for temperature tracking.

3. RS485 Long-Distance Bus

MAX485 differential serial transceiver ensures data reaches the local gateway across 1km+ without dependence on cellular networks.

Emergency Boil-Water & Filtration Protocol

1

Rolling boil

During an advisory, bring water to a rolling boil for 1 minute (or follow the specific instruction from your health authority). Boiling does not remove chemical contamination.

2

Optical & Cloth Filtration

If water is visibly cloudy, let it settle and strain through a clean cloth or coffee filter before disinfection. Filtration alone does not make unsafe water safe.

3

Follow official disinfection advice

Use only an approved water disinfectant and follow its label or local health-authority instructions. Do not guess bleach quantities; never mix bleach with other cleaners.

Infant formula during an advisory

Use commercially prepared sterile liquid formula if available. If powdered formula must be used, follow current advice from your health professional or local health authority about safe water preparation; never use water covered by a chemical contamination warning. Contact a clinic for infant-specific guidance.

4

Safe Storage

Store boiled water in clean, covered food-grade containers away from direct heat or light.
